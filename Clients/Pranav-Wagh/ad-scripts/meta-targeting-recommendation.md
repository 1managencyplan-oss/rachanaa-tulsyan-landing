# Meta Ads Targeting Recommendation — Zero to Hero Academy

---

## CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVE
Lead Generation (Meta Lead Forms or Landing Page clicks to https://live.zerotoheroacademy.co.in/live-fb/)

---

## BUDGET ALLOCATION (₹4–5 lakh/month)
| Campaign | Monthly Budget | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Broad Maharashtra (Testing) | ₹1,00,000 | Find cheapest CPL at scale |
| Interest-Based (Tier 1) | ₹1,50,000 | Qualified dropshipping intent leads |
| Lookalike Audiences | ₹1,00,000 | Scale from existing buyers/leads |
| Retargeting | ₹50,000 | Re-engage website visitors, video viewers |
| Marathi Language Test | ₹50,000–1,00,000 | Marathi-copy creatives vs Hindi |

---

## PRIMARY TARGETING

### Geography
- **Primary:** Maharashtra (Pune, Mumbai, Nashik, Aurangabad, Nagpur, Kolhapur, Solapur)
- **Secondary Test:** Goa, Karnataka (Marathi-speaking diaspora)

### Age & Gender
- Age: 22–40
- Gender: All (male skew likely — test both)

---

## INTEREST TARGETING (Ad Sets)

### Ad Set 1 — Entrepreneurship & Side Income
- Entrepreneurship, Small Business, Self Employment
- Passive Income, Make Money Online, Online Business
- E-commerce, Shopify, WooCommerce

### Ad Set 2 — Dropshipping & Digital Skills
- Dropshipping, Print on Demand
- Digital Marketing, Online Marketing
- Facebook Ads, Instagram for Business

### Ad Set 3 — Job Seekers / Career Changers
- Job Hunting, Career Development, Freelancing
- MBA, Business Administration (frustrated professionals)
- Work from Home

### Ad Set 4 — Marathi Audience (Language Targeting)
- Language: Marathi
- Region: Maharashtra
- Interests: any of the above
- Use Marathi-language ad creatives for this set

---

## LOOKALIKE AUDIENCES (once data builds)
- 1% Lookalike of past course buyers (upload customer list)
- 1% Lookalike of lead form submitters
- 2–3% Lookalike for scaling

---

## RETARGETING AUDIENCES
- Website visitors (last 30 days) — Meta Pixel
- Video viewers (50%+ watch time on ads)
- Lead form openers who didn't submit
- Instagram/Facebook page engagers (last 60 days)

---

## CREATIVE RECOMMENDATIONS
- **Video:** Use testimonial clips + Pranav on camera (Marathi or Hindi-Marathi mix)
- **Static:** Bold text hooks + before/after store screenshots
- **Carousel:** Step-by-step "How it works" (product → store → orders → income)
- **Reels:** Short 15–30 sec punchy hooks for Instagram

---

## CPL TARGET
- Goal: ₹60–70 per lead
- If CPL > ₹100 after 3 days: pause ad set, test new creative or audience
- If CPL < ₹50: scale budget 20% every 3 days

---

## TESTING FRAMEWORK (First 2 Weeks)
1. Run 3–4 ad sets simultaneously with ₹500–1,000/day each
2. Test 2 creatives per ad set (different hooks)
3. After 7 days: kill bottom performers, double budget on winners
4. Week 3: introduce lookalike audiences
