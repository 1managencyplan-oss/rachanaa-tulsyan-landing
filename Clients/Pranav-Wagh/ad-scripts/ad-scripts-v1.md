## COPY NOTES FOR CREATIVE TEAM
- All hooks must appear as on-screen text (first 3 sec) — many users watch on mute
- Use student result screenshots/testimonials as B-roll
- Keep CTA button text: "Free Syllabus मिळवा" or "Batch Details बघा"
- Primary color palette: professional — dark blue/orange or brand colors
- Add WhatsApp CTA option as secondary CTA for warm audiences
